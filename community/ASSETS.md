# Asset provenance

The landing page showcases three stories observed in the public Picsart TV stage app on September 16, 2026: Dracula, The endless void., and The City Below. Titles, premises and descriptions are based on the public catalog, studio collection and story details.

## Product screenshots

`live-story-map.png`, `live-watch.png`, `live-studios.png` and `live-catalog.png` were captured from the public stage app through the browser tool. No private account information was used. Screenshots are preserved as captured; the first three appear in the expandable product walkthrough. The catalog capture is retained in the source assets.

The two Dracula branch descriptions and contributor handles in `stories.js` were transcribed from the live story map. These are real existing continuations, not suggested prompts or simulated generation. No community metrics are invented.

The original fictional sample art remains in the source archive but is no longer referenced by this page or its portable build. The Picsart logo and font definitions are unchanged.
